Taxi Shift privacy policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026. Applies to Taxi Shift, Android package com.riviproj.taxishift, and the packaged Windows game.

Offline gameplay

The packaged game runs on your device. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, multiplayer servers, location collection, or in-app purchases. The game does not collect or share personal data.

Progress on your device

Earned money, completed trips, unlocked cars, customization and race records are saved locally. These records are not sent to us. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app can remove them. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app.

Permissions and diagnostics

No camera, microphone, contacts or location access is requested. Optional sound plays through your device. The diagnostic page displays browser and graphics information locally. It does not upload reports automatically; you may choose to copy and share a report when asking for support.

Store services

Google Play and your operating system may process information under their own policies, including installation and platform crash diagnostics. We do not operate those services.

Children

The game does not request personal information from players of any age. It includes fictional taxi driving and arcade races. The store listing supplies the applicable content rating.

Contact

For support and privacy questions, use the developer contact email shown in Taxi Shift’s Google Play listing. The release owner must supply that public contact before publishing.

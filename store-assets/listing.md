# Taxi Shift

Short description (under 80 characters):

Drive, drift and earn your way through Sunbay in an offline taxi adventure.

Full description:

Your next adventure is a fare away. Explore Sunbay City, pick up passengers, and take them to their destinations. Earn fares, unlock new rides, and make your taxi your own.

• Four distinct cars: start with the City Cab and unlock three more.
• Free paint, wheel and racing-stripe customization using in-game options.
• Three timed checkpoint races with wide gates and turn guidance.
• Arcade drifting with tire smoke, skid marks and drift scores.
• Chase and driver-seat cameras.
• Landscape touch controls for steering, acceleration, braking, drifting and boost.
• Offline play and progress saved on your device.

No accounts, ads or in-app purchases. Earnings and unlocks are part of gameplay.
